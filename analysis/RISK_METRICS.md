# IL, LVR, and Predictable Loss definitions

This document fixes the notation, empirical mesh, signs, and dynamics used by
the WETH/USDT LP-risk build. Fees and gas are excluded from all three measures.

## Common notation

- `P_t`: internal Uniswap pool price in USDT per WETH.
- `S_t`: strict-prior Binance ETHUSDT external price.
- `x(P), y(P)`: fee-exclusive WETH and USDT inventory reconstructed from the
  position's liquidity and range.
- `V(P) = x(P)P + y(P)`: LP value at its internal price. Within the range,
  `V'(P) = x(P)` and `V''(P) = x'(P) < 0`.
- `W(P,S) = x(P)S + y(P)`: the same LP inventory marked at the external price.
- `H(S) = x_0 S + y_0`: the actual deposited-token HODL benchmark.
- `V_0 = H(S_0)`: initial capital used for common loss-positive normalization.

Entry and exit inventory use actual Mint and Burn token amounts. Intermediate
inventory uses exact `sqrt_price_x96` pool states.

## Impermanent Loss

The paper-standard signed ratio and the chart's loss-positive amount are

\[
IL_t^{signed}=\frac{W(P_t,S_t)-H(S_t)}{H(S_t)}, \qquad
L_t^{IL}=H(S_t)-W(P_t,S_t).
\]

The exact discrete change used to interpret a path is

\[
\Delta L_j^{IL}=x_0\Delta S_j-
  \left[W(P_j,S_j)-W(P_{j-1},S_{j-1})\right],
\qquad
L_t^{IL}=\sum_{j\le t}\Delta L_j^{IL}.
\]

IL is an endpoint state difference, not an intrinsically monotone cost flow;
its increments can have either sign. The implementation evaluates the state
difference directly at every requested timestamp, which is algebraically
equivalent to the telescoping sum above and avoids accumulated rounding error.

## Loss-versus-Rebalancing

For every in-scope pool Swap `k`, in blockchain log order,

\[
\Delta LVR_k=-\left(S_k\Delta x_k+\Delta y_k\right), \qquad
LVR_t=\sum_{k:t_k\le t}\Delta LVR_k.
\]

`S_k` is the Binance close exactly one second before the block timestamp.
This is an empirical self-financing rebalancing gap. It is not clipped: pool-CEX
basis and discrete timing can make an increment, or even a cumulative value,
negative. CEX quadratic-variation estimates on 1-second, 5-second, and 1-minute
meshes remain non-negative robustness measures rather than the primary LVR.

## Predictable Loss

### Primary block-end mesh

Let `b=1,...,B` index Ethereum blocks containing at least one in-scope Swap
while the position is alive. Let `bar(P)_0` be the pool state immediately before
entry and `bar(P)_b` the state after the last in-scope Swap in block `b`. The
primary Convexity Cost increment is

\[
C_b=V(\bar P_{b-1})+x(\bar P_{b-1})
  (\bar P_b-\bar P_{b-1})-V(\bar P_b)\ge 0.
\]

Using one endpoint per block prevents a transient intra-block excursion and
its reversal from being treated as two separately hedgeable market moves. The
former every-Swap calculation is retained under
`pl_swap_event_*` as a microstructure/mesh sensitivity.

Let `G(s,t)` be the frozen SOFR ACT/360 gross factor. Loss-positive Predictable
Loss follows

\[
D_b=G(t_{b-1},t_b)D_{b-1}+C_b,
\]

and at any evaluation time `t` after the latest included block,

\[
D_t=G(0,t)\sum_{b:t_b\le t}\frac{C_b}{G(0,t_b)}.
\]

The reported decomposition is

\[
\text{Convexity Cost}_t=\sum_{b:t_b\le t}C_b,\qquad
\text{Opportunity Cost}_t=D_t-\sum_{b:t_b\le t}C_b,
\]

\[
\text{PL loss}_t=D_t
  =\text{Convexity Cost}_t+\text{Opportunity Cost}_t,
\qquad
PL_t^{paper}=-D_t.
\]

SOFR therefore grows only an already-created replication gap. If every `C_b`
is zero, PL and Opportunity Cost remain zero even when the interest rate is
positive.

## Continuous-time dynamics and their relationship

The following identities describe the ideal frictionless limit in which the
arbitraged pool price and external price coincide continuously, `P_t=S_t`.
They are interpretation equations, not a replacement for the empirical
discrete estimators when pool-CEX basis is present.

By Itô's formula, the fee-exclusive LP and HODL dynamics are

\[
dV_t^{LP}=x(S_t)dS_t+\frac12V''(S_t)d[S]_t,
\qquad dH_t=x_0dS_t.
\]

Consequently, for loss-positive dollar IL,

\[
dL_t^{IL}=(x_0-x(S_t))dS_t
  -\frac12V''(S_t)d[S]_t.
\]

The continuous LVR and Convexity Cost flows are

\[
dLVR_t=dC_t=-\frac12V''(S_t)d[S]_t\ge0.
\]

Loss-positive PL and its Opportunity Cost satisfy

\[
dD_t=dC_t+r_tD_tdt,\qquad dO_t=r_tD_tdt,
\]

so that

\[
D_t=B_t\int_0^t B_u^{-1}dC_u,
\qquad B_t=\exp\!\left(\int_0^t r_u du\right).
\]

The ideal-limit relationship is therefore

\[
dL_t^{IL}=(x_0-x(S_t))dS_t+dLVR_t,
\]

and, when `r=0`, `dPL_loss=dLVR=dC`. IL additionally contains directional
inventory exposure, while LVR/Convexity Cost measure the adverse curvature or
quadratic-variation component. With positive rates, PL adds financing of the
gap already generated by that curvature.

These dynamics can be integrated for a single position. In the implemented
time series, IL is the exact state difference, LVR is the prefix sum of Swap
increments, and PL is the SOFR-grown prefix sum of block-end Convexity Cost
increments. Differences between empirical LVR and PL are expected because LVR
uses every actual inventory transfer marked at `S_k`, while primary PL uses the
block-end internal-price mesh.
